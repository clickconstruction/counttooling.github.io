Not everything on a takeoff is a count. Questions, flags, and review marks belong on the sheet too — where they're impossible to lose.

![A highlight flagging a region and a note pinned where the question applies — both right on the sheet, neither touching the counts.](/guides/img/annotate.png)

## Highlights

The **Highlight** [[highlight]] tool (`H`) draws a translucent rectangle over a region — "look at this," without touching any numbers. Two clicks: corner, corner. Use them to flag conflicts, unclear areas, or scope boundaries; they can be [bundled into the exported PDF](/guides/reports-and-exports/) so reviewers see them too.

## Notes

The **Note** [[note]] tool (`N`) pins text to a spot: click, type, done. Notes render in red on the sheet and are made to be worked with:

- **Move** them by dragging; **resize** the width and the text size with their handles.
- **Edit** by double-clicking (or the right-click menu).
- Long text wraps automatically.

A question written where it applies beats a question in an email thread — and notes can be bundled into the PDF export, so the deliverable carries the conversation.

## The legend

The **legend** [[legend]] overlay is the on-sheet scoreboard: counts and lengths by type (and room volumes when you use the [Room Sizer](/guides/measuring-room-volumes/)), updated live. Drag it wherever it doesn't cover work; resize it by its corner. **Legend Settings** (right-click the legend button) control background opacity and color, border, text opacity, and overall scale — tune it to sit quietly on the sheet.

## Hide marks — the bare drawing

The eye button [[hide-marks]] in the header peels the entire overlay off — counters, lines, highlights, notes, legend — so the drawing underneath reads clean. Tap again to bring everything back. It's purely visual: no data changes, exports are unaffected, and it persists as you flip pages. [View-link](/guides/sharing-and-view-links/) recipients get the same toggle, and their choice is remembered per link.

## The grid

For layout-heavy review, the **Grid** [[grid]] overlay draws an adjustable real-unit grid over the sheet (spacing, origin, color, line style), with optional snapping while you draw. It's view-only — never exported — and needs the page scale set first.
