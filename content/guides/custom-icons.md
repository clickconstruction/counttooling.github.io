A takeoff you can *read* is a takeoff someone else can check. When a counter looks like the fixture it counts — a floor drain, a P-trap, a 45-elbow — the marked-up plan doubles as a legible drawing instead of a field of dots.

![The Create Counter dialog: ① name the counter, ② pick a built-in icon or upload your own, and ③ choose a color.](/guides/img/counter-create.png)

## The built-in library

Create a counter [[counter]] and the icon grid offers the bundled set: general symbols plus a library of real trade fixtures. Use the search box to filter by name. Every icon takes the counter's color, so the same symbol can serve several types.

## Upload your own SVG

The icon grid's **+ Upload** cell accepts your own SVG files:

- The SVG needs at least one `path`, `rect`, `circle`, `ellipse`, or `line` element; a `viewBox` is recommended. Multiple shapes are merged into one symbol.
- Uploaded icons appear under **Custom Icons** in the picker, available to every counter you make.
- They're included when you export a canvas, so a takeoff shared as a file still renders your symbols.
- The **Custom Icons** label in the picker opens a tips dialog with the format details.

## Manage the library

**Manage Icons** (Settings gear → **Advanced** → Manage Icons) lists every icon. No account needed — the Settings gear opens whether or not you're signed in, on desktop and on a phone or tablet alike.

![The Manage Icons dialog: rename any icon so it matches your vocabulary, and reorder the picker so your daily icons sit on top.](/guides/img/manage-icons.png)

- **Rename** any icon — built-in or uploaded — so it matches your vocabulary.
- **Reorder** so your daily icons sit at the top of the picker.
- **Edit mode** lets you select uploaded icons and delete the ones you no longer use.

## Your icons follow your account

Uploaded icons are stored per browser — and when you save your [Artboard](/guides/artboard-and-palette-insights/) to the cloud, your custom icon library rides along, so signing in on another device brings your symbols with your palette.
