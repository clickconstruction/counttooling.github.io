The fastest way to learn a takeoff tool is to do a takeoff. CountTooling teaches itself inside the app, on sample sheets it opens for you, so nothing you try touches a real project.

![The Learn menu: the three five-minute trade tours at the top, and the list of short lessons, one per part of the app, with a tick beside each one finished.](/guides/img/learn-menu.png)

## How a lesson works

Open the app with nothing loaded and click **lesson** on the empty canvas, or open [Learn](/app/?learn=1) directly. It is also under **Project Settings** → **Help** → **lessons**.

- A card beside the screen says what to do, one action per line, naming each control the way it looks on screen, and lights the control it means.
- Every step **checks what you actually did**. The Scale lesson does not move on until the 12'-0" dimension really measures 12'-0".
- Every step has a **Do it for me** button that does the same thing through the app, so you can watch a step you do not want to work out.
- **Back** goes back, the card drags out of the way by its top edge, and the × leaves whenever you like. A finished lesson is ticked on your device, and the menu lights the next one.

If your own plan is open, a lesson asks before it closes it. Lessons never run on a project saved to the cloud.

## Start with your trade: the five-minute tours

Each tour is one small takeoff from scale to hand-off, fourteen steps on the design-build sample plan.

- [Plumbing tour](/app/?tour=plumbing): prove the scale, count a restroom, chain a lav battery with its riser, hangers from the rulebook, a typical floor, an RFI, the proof, the hand-off. Read along in [Doing a plumbing takeoff](/guides/plumbing-takeoff/).
- [Electrical tour](/app/?tour=electrical): devices with mount heights, conduit with its conductors, chained runs with the vertical, a circuit, Bid Check. Read along in [Doing an electrical takeoff](/guides/electrical-takeoff/).
- [HVAC tour](/app/?tour=hvac): a room and its CFM target, diffusers, a system, a main that sizes itself, pounds of duct. Read along in [Doing an HVAC takeoff](/guides/hvac-takeoff/).

## Then every part of the app: the lessons

Two or three minutes each, in any order. They run on three sample sheets: a restaurant plumbing plan, an enlarged restroom plan with a detail at another scale that is typical of four, and a fixture schedule that was scanned sideways.

1. [Sheets](/app/?lesson=plans): trim a set, find a sheet, turn it, name it, jump between the sheets with marks.
2. [Scale](/app/?lesson=scale): a scale per sheet, prove it, and a scale zone over a detail drawn at another scale.
3. [Counting](/app/?lesson=counting): a counter, a count, and Quick Keys on the number row.
4. [Measuring](/app/?lesson=measuring): snap, trace a run, elbows counted from its bends, a riser.
5. [Chain and child counts](/app/?lesson=chain): fixtures and pipe in one pass, hangers from the rule, the § chip.
6. [Repeats](/app/?lesson=repeats): count a typical once and multiply it.
7. [Organizing](/app/?lesson=organize): groups, the sidebar filter, a layer for an alternate, Hide marks.
8. [Fixing mistakes](/app/?lesson=fixing): undo, delete one mark, change a whole type, clear an area.
9. [Notes and questions](/app/?lesson=notes): notes, RFI flags, highlights.
10. [Check and prove](/app/?lesson=check): Bid Check, the proof breakdown, the on-sheet legend.
11. [Deliverables](/app/?lesson=deliver): the report, marked-up PDFs, the hand-off to the bid, an email summary.
12. [Working faster](/app/?lesson=speed): the keyboard map, the zoom rail, right-click settings.
13. [Saving, sharing and your bids](/app/?lesson=cloud): a guided read of the signed-in half.

When you are ready for a real plan, [How to do a takeoff from a PDF](/guides/how-to-do-a-pdf-takeoff/) is the same workflow on your own sheet.
