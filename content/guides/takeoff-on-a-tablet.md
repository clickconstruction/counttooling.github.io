Field questions get answered where the work is — a basement, a mechanical room, a trailer with bad wifi. CountTooling is built to run there: touch-first, installable, and fully functional offline.

![CountTooling on a tablet — the full toolbar, counters, and summary alongside a marked-up plan.](/guides/img/on-a-tablet.png)

## Touch that actually works

- **One finger pans, pinch zooms** — plan navigation feels like a map app, and it stays smooth on big, dense sheets.
- **Long-press** any mark for its context menu — the same edit and delete options as right-click on desktop.
- **The aim loupe**: press and hold with a placing tool active and a magnifier appears above your finger with a crosshair, so you can set a counter or line endpoint *precisely* even though your fingertip covers the spot. Slide to aim, release to place. The crosshair rides just above your fingertip on purpose — that offset is what keeps your finger from hiding the exact point you're placing on, so aim with the crosshair, not the finger.
- The **zoom rail** — tap the zoom % in the footer for a big vertical slider with labeled ticks, much friendlier than pinching to an exact level:

![The zoom rail on a tablet: a large log-scale slider with labeled ticks and a draggable readout thumb, on the right edge where a thumb can reach it.](/guides/img/zoom-rail.png)
- On a phone-sized screen the header folds its secondary actions into a **menu drawer**, and the sidebar slides in from the left — everything's reachable, nothing's cramped.

## Install it like an app

Add CountTooling to the home screen ([how](/guides/working-offline-and-installing/)) for a full-screen, one-tap app. On iPhone/iPad: Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. On Android or desktop Chrome: the browser's **Install** option.

## Offline is the point

Once a takeoff is loaded, **everything works with zero signal** — counting, measuring, marking up. Changes save locally every few seconds and [sync automatically](/guides/how-your-work-is-saved/) when you're back on a connection. One iPhone note: the installed app keeps separate storage from Safari, so open your takeoff once in the installed app *while online* before heading somewhere dark.

## A field workflow that holds up

- Start the takeoff at your desk, and it picks up on the tablet — same project, synced.
- Verify a dimension on site with the **Measure** [[measure]] tool; two taps, real feet.
- Received a [view link](/guides/sharing-and-view-links/) instead of an account? Viewers get pan, zoom, layers, **Hide marks** [[hide-marks]] to read the bare drawing — and can even set a page scale that shares back to the project.
