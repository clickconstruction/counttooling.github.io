When the power goes out, the exit signs and the emergency lights have to keep the way out lit for an hour and a half. On a restaurant that source is almost always a battery in each fixture (unit equipment), fed from the same circuit as the normal lighting in the area, ahead of any switch.

On a bid that makes the Type X and Type EM fixtures more than a fixture: a battery, a test switch, and an unswitched circuit to each one. The building code says where the lights go (IBC 1008, its own rule); this rule says how long they must last.

## What the app does with it

Nothing yet. The electrical course teaches it on a card (Chapter 3, Which rooms switch themselves off?). The app counts the fixtures you mark; it does not know which are emergency or check their circuit.

## Verify against your edition

The duration is written for every emergency source in 700.12, and the paragraph for unit equipment has moved letters between editions. A tester signs the 90 minutes against the adopted edition before this rule is applied.
