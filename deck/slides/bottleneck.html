The problem

A shop can only bid as many jobs as its estimators can count.

Slow

Count every fixture. Size every pipe. Look up every code. Price every line. Write the letter. Days of a senior person's time, per bid.

Scarce

75,000 estimators for 588,000 shops. Most shops have none, so they never bid plan work at all.

Risky

Every plan set has mistakes. Miss one and the shop pays for it in the field. Catching it takes your most experienced person.

The work goes to whoever gets a good number out first. Today that is whoever has the most estimators.

Estimator and shop counts from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, 2024.

Say the problem in the buyer's words before any product. The bottleneck is people: a bid is days of a senior estimator's time, most shops have no estimator at all, and the mistakes in the plans land on whoever missed them. Everything that follows is the answer to this slide.
