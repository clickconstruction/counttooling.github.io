How each row is built. 1: 48,816 plumbing, HVAC and electrical shops with 10+ people; low is seat pricing like STACK ($5k to $50k a year by size), high adds robot bids at $250 each and Canada, the UK and Australia. 2: 170,952 shops under 10 people that never bid plan work; 10 to 15 robot bids a year at $250 to $350. 3: the same 48,816 shops at $20k low and $60k high a year, under what ServiceTitan and Procore get per customer. 4: a 0.5% to 1.5% fee on plan-and-spec work won, 30% to 40% of the $390B the three trades bill. 5: all 588,440 specialty trade shops, same size mix, same prices as path 1. 6: 0.3% to 1% of the revenue that flows through customers on path 3. 7: 57,264 commercial GCs buying robot trade numbers, $10k to $35k a year.
