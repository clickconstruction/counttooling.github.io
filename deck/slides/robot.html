What is new

The robot is a user.

1 · It bids first, blind

The robot bids the same job from the same plans and locks its number before the person has one. It cannot copy.

2 · The person bids, then looks

They write down their number, then open the robot's and compare, while the bid can still change.

3 · The app keeps score

When the bid goes out, the difference is written on the bid. A person signs off, fixes what is wrong, or redoes any step by hand.
