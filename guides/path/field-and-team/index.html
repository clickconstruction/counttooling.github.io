On the job site & with your team

Offline, on a tablet, and shared without stepping on each other.

New here? Start with The Basics first — this track assumes it.

Install the app, work with no signal, mark up on a tablet, share projects safely, and know exactly how your work is saved.

1 Installing the app and working offline Add CountTooling to your device for a one-tap icon, and keep working on a takeoff with no connection — built for the job site, not just the office. 2 Takeoffs on a tablet in the field Touch gestures, the aim loupe for fingertip precision, and full offline support — how CountTooling works on a tablet at the job site, not just at a desk. 3 Sharing takeoffs and view links Work together without stepping on each other — share projects with viewers or editors, check out to edit, and send email-gated view-only links that need no sign-in. 4 How your work is saved (and how to tell) Auto-save every few seconds, local backups on your device, and the Save Status bell — what each state means and what to do on the rare yellow day.
