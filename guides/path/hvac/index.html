HVAC track

Room volumes first — then equipment and duct runs.

New here? Start with The Basics first — this track assumes it.

Turn rooms into areas and air volumes for sizing, then count equipment and measure duct runs across mixed-scale sheets.

1 Measuring room volumes with the Room Sizer Draw room boxes, give each a ceiling height, and get floor area and air volume per room — the numbers behind airflow, air changes, and equipment sizing. 2 Doing an HVAC takeoff Room volumes for airflow and equipment sizing, counters for diffusers and equipment, and duct runs measured off the plan — HVAC estimating on the PDF. 3 Scale zones and multiply zones Handle multi-scale sheets and repeated areas — give a region its own scale, or multiply everything inside a boundary so typical floors and units count correctly.
