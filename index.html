Frequently asked questions

What trades is it for?

Plumbing, electrical, and HVAC. Counting fixtures, devices, diffusers, and equipment; measuring pipe, conduit, and duct; and the trade math on top: hangers, conductors and voltage drop, room airflow and duct weight.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CountTooling runs in any modern web browser, with nothing to download or update. You can optionally install it as an app for a home-screen icon.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once a takeoff is loaded you can keep counting, measuring, and marking up with no connection. It auto-saves with local backups and syncs back up when you reconnect.

Can I export to my pricing software?

Yes. Copy a plumbing or HVAC takeoff straight into PipeTooling, open an electrical takeoff in TakeoffTooling, copy a summary for email, or export a marked-up PDF with the report included.

Does it work on a tablet?

Yes. Single-finger pan, pinch to zoom, long-press for the menu, and an aim loupe for precise placement with a finger.

How is it different from desktop estimating software?

It runs in the browser with nothing to install, works on any device and even offline, and exports into your pricing workflow, versus heavy Windows-only desktop tools.
