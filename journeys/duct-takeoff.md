## Naive attempt

Booted cold as a design-build estimator with the guide half-remembered. The gate, the scale and the rooms went exactly as J7 promised (~6 actions per room, then 3 more per room to give it a type — I did not expect to come back for that). Devices were quick once I found the CFM field; the icon that got picked for me was invisible (the "Icon" panel showed plumbing glyphs with nothing selected — I created it anyway and got the right diffuser). Hunting the RTU glyph cost ~8 actions (Custom Icons sub-tab, scroll two trades down). The real stall was the system: the New Duct Run dialog told me to "edit in Groups" and there was no Groups anywhere in the sidebar; the sidebar gear row was missing signed-out; I found it under the header ⚙ → Project Settings → a toggle the guide never names (~10 actions; a real user reads "edit in Groups" three times and gives up on systems — the duct still traces fine without one, but capacity, ESP, static path and flex-per-system never light up). Tracing itself was the best part: two clicks, a suggestion under the cursor, `S`, tap, Enter. Deck height turned out to live at the bottom of a schedule I couldn't open until a run existed, so the RTU riser on the main never happened. Schedule, Bid Check and the export gate all read like a bid. ~70 actions to the exported PDF including the two detours; goal reached.

- duct-takeoff-by-the-pound.md never says the Groups section is off until Project Settings → "Use groups in this project" (header ⚙); "edit in Groups" and "the Groups list" assume it is on screen (Friction #1).

- No guide mentions the missing hotkey (every other tool is listed with its key in working-faster-with-the-keyboard.md).
