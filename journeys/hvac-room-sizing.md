## Current route (walked 2026-08-02)

9 steps, 3 decision points on this plan (the third decision — trust the preset's sheet
correction or not — only exists on non-standard sheets, but that is exactly what the
sample plan and any re-boxed/compressed PDF is).

1. Set the page scale first (the tool won't start without one — #setScaleFirstModal toast, verified on both the cube button and V, with and without a PDF loaded). The Set Scale panel opens on the presets tab; on this sheet it also shows "⚠ This page isn't a standard sheet size" with a correction dropdown pre-set to its best guess (ANSI D). **Decision:** accept the correction, switch it to "Non-standard — don't correct", or use Select two points. Accepting the guess silently applied a 0.375× correction — Office 101 later read 55'-10" instead of ~23' (Friction #3). At 900px viewport height the panel's title and its "Select two points / Architectural & Engineering" tabs render above the top edge and cannot be clicked or scrolled to (Friction #2), so the recovery that actually works on-screen is the correction dropdown → "Non-standard — don't correct" → re-click the preset.
2. Pick the Room Sizer tool — cube button or V. (Once a scale exists the toolbar Set Scale button disappears; the sidebar scale readout top-left is the re-edit path.)
3. Click one corner of the room (rubber-band preview with live L×W readout begins; status bar walks you through: "Click first corner" → "Click second corner"). A drag-release gesture is NOT a box: it commits only the first corner, and the follow-up click can produce a 0'-0"×0'-0" box whose dialog still opens (Friction #5).
4. Click the opposite corner — the Room Size dialog (#roomBoxModal) opens with the Length/Width/area table (23'-11" × 20'-1" · 479 ft² for Office 101).
5. Enter the ceiling height in feet (`9.5` and `9'6` both parse) or tap a recent-height chip (chips are most-recent-first, max 5, persisted in localStorage `recentRoomHeights`). Leaving it empty makes Apply a silent no-op (Friction #4).
6. **Decision:** pick the room under "Add to Room" (each row shows running area | volume) or "+ New room" and name it (placeholder "e.g. Office 101"). The last-used room row comes preselected, the last height pre-filled.
7. Click Apply — the box commits in its room's color with name + L×W×H label; the tool stays armed.
8. Repeat two clicks per additional box; for an L-shaped/split room draw more boxes onto the same room — its sidebar row sums them (Break Room 102 from two boxes: 442 ft² · 4,425 ft³).
9. Read totals: Rooms sidebar section (per-room ft²/ft³, per-box rows with ✕), legend room rows, and the report's Room Volumes table via Show Report → "Takeoff Report for this Canvas Only". **But on a rooms-only takeoff step 9 dead-ends**: Show Report / Export PDFs / Copy Summary stay hidden until at least one counter mark or line exists (Friction #1). With one counter placed, the report renders Room | Area (ft²) | Volume (ft³) | Pages exactly as documented, and Copy Summary's text gains a "--- Rooms ---" block.
